🏨 AtliQ Grands Hospitality Insights – Power BI Dashboard 📊
🚀 Welcome to My Interactive Hospitality Analytics Project! 🚀

This project showcases a dynamic Power BI dashboard developed for AtliQ Grands, a premium five-star hotel chain in India. The goal was to help their revenue management team make data-driven decisions amid growing competition and strategic challenges.

📌 Project Overview:
Due to declining market share and lack of an internal analytics team, AtliQ Grands outsourced their data intelligence needs. This dashboard was built to provide clear, actionable insights into revenue, occupancy, booking trends, and performance by city, hotel, room class, and booking platform.

🔍 Features of the Dashboard:
💰 Executive KPIs: Revenue, Bookings, Cancellations, Avg. Customer Rating

🧳 Booking Platform Analysis: Revenue and performance by platform (Direct, OTA, Corporate)

🏨 Hotel & City Performance: Breakdown of revenue and occupancy for each hotel and city

🛏 Room Class Insights: Revenue contribution by room categories (Standard, Deluxe, Suite)

📈 Performance Metrics: ADR (Average Daily Rate), RevPAR (Revenue per Available Room), Occupancy Rate

📊 Trend Analysis: Week-over-week trends in bookings, revenue, and cancellations

🎛 Interactive Filters: Hotel, City, Room Class, and Booking Platform filters for deep-dive analysis

🛠 Tools and Techniques Used:
Power BI for data visualization and interactivity

DAX for custom KPIs and measures

Data Modeling & Relationships

Data Cleaning & Transformation using Power Query

📈 Key Insights from the Dashboard:
🔎 Identifies underperforming hotels and booking platforms

📊 Highlights profitable room classes and top-performing cities

📉 Tracks occupancy dips and cancellation spikes to inform strategy

🧠 Empowers the team to take proactive, data-driven decisions
