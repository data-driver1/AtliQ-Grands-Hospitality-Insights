🔍 AtliQ Grands Hospitality Insights Dashboard – Power BI Project
This project presents a comprehensive Power BI dashboard developed for AtliQ Grands, a leading chain of five-star hotels in India. The dashboard offers data-driven insights to help AtliQ Grands address performance challenges and regain market share in the competitive hospitality industry.

🏨 Problem Statement
AtliQ Grands has been operating in the luxury/business hotel segment for over two decades. In recent years, the company began to lose market share and revenue due to stronger strategic execution by competitors and ineffective internal decision-making.

To counter this decline, the Managing Director initiated a strategic plan to integrate Business and Data Intelligence into the organization. However, the company lacked an internal analytics team. As a solution, the Revenue Management Team onboarded a third-party service provider to generate insights from their historical data.

📊 Project Goals
The primary objective was to deliver a user-friendly, interactive dashboard to empower leadership with key business insights. This dashboard helps track KPIs, monitor performance across cities, hotels, and booking platforms, and uncover revenue opportunities.

The Power BI dashboard equips AtliQ Grands’ leadership and revenue team with:

📊 Actionable insights to identify underperforming hotels, cities, and booking platforms
